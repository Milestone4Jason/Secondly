# Secondly — Business Plan

## Mission
Build a free-to-start eSIM service with a real Italian mobile number and transparent data allowances.

## Planned plans

### Secondly Free
- 5 GB per 5-week cycle.
- Target: one real Italian +39 mobile number.
- eSIM activation on supported devices, subject to provider compatibility.

### Rewarded data
- 100 MB for each successfully verified rewarded advertisement.
- Maximum 10 rewarded ads per user per day (up to 1 GB/day).
- Rewards are credited only after server-side verification.
- Ad availability and mobile data costs are not guaranteed.

### Founder
- 20 GB per day for the designated founder account.
- Founder privileges must be assigned by a trusted server-side administrator, never by trusting a client-supplied username or role.

## Revenue hypotheses
- Rewarded advertising.
- Optional data add-ons or paid plans.
- Sponsorships and business partnerships.

Advertising revenue must be tested against wholesale data costs before committing to the reward economics.

## Dependencies before launch
1. Adult-led legal and commercial arrangements.
2. A network/eSIM provider supporting Italian mobile numbers, voice, SMS, data and eSIM provisioning.
3. Applicable Italian telecom authorization/notification and customer-identification compliance.
4. Privacy, security, customer support and emergency-calling arrangements.
5. Sustainable wholesale pricing and funding.

## Current status
This is a product proposal, not an active telecom service. No real phone numbers, eSIM profiles, data bundles or ad rewards are currently issued.
